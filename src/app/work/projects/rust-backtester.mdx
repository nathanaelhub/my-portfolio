## The brief

Most backtests flatter the strategy: same-day fills, no costs, parameters tuned on the whole
history. This one is built to make those mistakes impossible or visible.

## The build

- **No look-ahead by construction**: a strategy sees bars up to today's close and fills at the next
  open. A test scrambles every bar after day *k* and checks nothing up to day *k* changes; an engine
  that fills on the signal's own day fails three tests.
- **Costs and dividends**: commission and slippage on every fill, whole shares, dividends paid in
  cash on the ex-date to the shares held the night before.
- **Checked against an outside source**: buy-and-hold SPY over 33.7 years comes within 0.003% a year
  of Yahoo's independently adjusted close.
- **A ratatui terminal UI**: arrow keys change the parameters and the backtest reruns on each key
  press; `w` switches to walk-forward.

## What I learned

Tuning overstates: on average about 40% of the in-sample Sharpe didn't carry into the next year.
Walk-forward trend-following never beat holding on return (×9.0 against ×6.6 on SPY). What it did
deliver was smaller drawdowns, −32% against −55% on SPY. The honest result was the less exciting one.

## Stack

Rust, ratatui, crossterm, Yahoo Finance daily data.
